Support

Independent app by Aarav Raheja, United Kingdom

RouteReady helps learner drivers practise around real UK driving test centres — test-style routes with spoken directions, mock tests that score themselves, and a record of what to work on next.

Need help?

Email aaravraheja99@gmail.com. It reaches the person who builds the app, and you can expect a reply within a few days. Telling us your device and iOS version helps.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Inside the app: Account → Delete account → Delete my account. The screen lists exactly what will be removed before you confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period and nothing is kept in an archive.

If you would rather we did it, email us from the address on the account and we will action it within 30 days.

I signed in with Apple and my name is missing

Apple sends your name only the very first time you use Sign in with Apple for an app, and never again. If that first sign-in did not complete, the name never reaches us. You can set it yourself in the app under Account, which is the authoritative version anyway.

Can I use Hide My Email?

Yes. RouteReady works exactly the same with an Apple relay address, and we never see your real one.

Does the app track my location when it is closed?

No. Location is used only while the app is open and you are using it, and it is never stored on our servers. See the privacy policy for exactly what is and is not kept.

Are these the real driving test routes?

No, and nobody has them — the DVSA does not publish official test routes. RouteReady's routes are built around real test centres in the style examiners drive, so you practise the roads and the manoeuvres you are likely to meet. They are practice routes, not a leaked answer sheet.

The map or directions look wrong

Roads change and map data lags behind. Always drive to the road, the signs and the law in front of you, never to the app. If a route has a genuine problem, email us the centre and route name and we will look at it.
